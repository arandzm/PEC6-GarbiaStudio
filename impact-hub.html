Impact Hub

Designed for an office building at Vizcaya 12 in Madrid, this project delivers a multifunctional ground floor space adding significant value to building occupants and external users alike. The flexible layout effortlessly transitions between collaborative work hubs, rest areas, meeting rooms, and a versatile central auditorium.

The space is divided into two distinct zones: an open, multi-level event hall near the main entry with organic furniture transitions, and a private rear area equipped with movable partition walls to host tailored meetings, workshops, and catering services.
