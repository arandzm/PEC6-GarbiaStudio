Arima

Arima, which means "soul" in Basque, stems from the intention of endowing each garment with a distinct identity and spirit. It is a fictional brand where both the clothing pieces and the digital e-commerce experience were custom-designed.

In a world dominated by mass production, we chose to create unique pieces crafted locally in Spain using proximity fabrics, ensuring every detail stands out with character.
